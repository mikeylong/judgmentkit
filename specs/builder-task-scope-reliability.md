# Preserve the builder's task scope

A builder asks an agent to inspect or improve one part of an existing interface. Project documentation also describes other features, decisions, and effects. JudgmentKit must retain those sources without treating every described feature as the current task.

The CLEAR encounter exposed two failures: preview-and-cancel inherited the authority requirement for a separate version commit, and background discussion features competed with artifact inspection. The task was to understand a correction and cancel its preview with the saved artifact unchanged. Apply remained a separate human decision.

## Required behavior

1. An irreversible source boundary transfers to an activity claim through the irreversible action it describes. Shared words such as read, inspect, or render cannot transfer a commit requirement from another part of the excerpt.
2. An explicitly non-executing brief may explore inspection, comparison, or preview with no saved change. Background execution requirements do not expand that task. Its candidate must preserve the limit: active Apply, adopt, revert, commit, save, update, replace, write, merge, or other protected effects disable the exemption, including effects in responsibilities and state changes. Declared participants may qualify inspection actions. Domain vocabulary and warnings are not active actions. A source saying that Preview itself saves, commits, or deletes retains the authority requirement for Preview's effect.
3. Source effects remain applicable when described before the inspection operation or in passive voice. An operation retained in the brief, objective, outcomes, or other active candidate fields cannot disappear through omission from next actions. Standalone and combined Apply actions disable the exemption; explicitly negated effects preserve it. A vague brief retains source-only protected boundaries for the activity's participants and objects. An executing brief cannot escape review through a euphemistic candidate. Sensitive disclosure and safety requirements remain applicable even to non-executing tasks.
4. Explicitly secondary conversation turns cannot become the primary activity through incidental message, reply, and continuity terms. Contradictory explicit primary conversation evidence remains a routing conflict.
5. Exact attributed source continues across activity review, workflow, and handoff. These scope rules grant no authority to Apply or accept an implementation. Artifact Inspector still requires trusted interactive attestation for implementation acceptance.
